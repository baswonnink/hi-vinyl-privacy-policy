Common questions

How does my collection sync between devices?
Hi, Vinyl syncs automatically through your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed into iCloud on each device and have iCloud Drive enabled — no separate account or sign-up needed.

How do I share my collection?
Open the Collection tab → gear icon → Collection sharing, and turn sharing on. You'll get a 6-character code to send to whoever you want to share with. They can enter it under "Collection sharing" in their own copy of the app to view your collection and, if you allow it, suggest additions.

How do I cancel or manage my Pro subscription?
Hi, Vinyl Pro renews monthly and can be cancelled anytime via Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro access until the current period ends.

Where does my data live?
Locally on your device and in your own private iCloud — see the privacy policy for details, including what the optional sharing and Pro photo-recognition features send where.
